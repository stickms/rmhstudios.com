# Mobile UI audit — 2026-10-31

> Scope: every static `_site` route (128, 125 distinct after trailing-slash
> duplicates) at **390 × 844**, iPhone Safari UA, Midnight/graphite theme, signed
> in as a fresh user on an **empty database**. Measured in a real browser, not
> read from source. Findings are ordered by how many pages they touch.

## What was and was not covered

Covered: layout edges, gutters, tap-target sizes, text size, overflow, and a
screenshot of the top of every page, read side by side.

**Not** covered, so treat "no finding" with care:

- Populated states. The DB was empty, so most pages showed their empty state. Long
  lists, real content, and error states are unaudited.
- Dynamic routes (`$id`, `$slug`), admin, and the full-screen game/app tier.
- Desktop, 360px, Daylight, and `jelly motion: still`.
- Real iOS Safari. Chromium cannot reproduce Safari's toolbar behaviour (see §6).
- `pnpm audit:pages` (the existing edge/anatomy audit) reported **no anatomy
  deviations** and 17 edge findings not in the allowlist (`glass-scrim.fixed` on
  `/lists`, `/marathon`, `/rmh-datacenter`, `/study/browse`, `/user-builds/submit`,
  `/when`; `header.stm-topbar` on `/user-builds/`). It does not measure spacing,
  which is why this drift was invisible to it.

## 1. Double page gutter — 44 of 67 pages that have cards

The title sits at **16px** from the screen edge (116 of 125 pages, exactly). Cards
on 44 pages sit at **32px** (a few at 28 or 48). The shell frame already supplies the
page gutter (`--rad-gutter`, `components/radial/radial.css`, ~16px at 390), and these
pages add a second `px-4` on their own wrapper, so content is indented twice.
Measured: cards at 32px on **141** outermost surfaces vs 16px on 92.

**Root cause is the docs.** `docs/page-consistency.md` §2 and
`docs/design-language.md` (~line 869) both prescribe
`px-4 pt-4 pb-12` on a page's inner wrapper. Following the recipe produces the bug.
`rideshare/index.tsx:92` and `ventures.tsx:128` do exactly that.

Pages (L = measured left edge of cards):
`/achievements /connections/new /create(48) /create/earnings /creator-studio
/daily-mutator /developer /emoji-packs /exchange /help /homes /homes/manage
/homes/rent-split /homes/saved /homes/submit /marathon /ranked /recap /rideshare
/rideshare/ride /rings /rmh-datacenter /rmh-datacenter/{contact,facilities,network,
platform,power} /rmhbox-packs /rmhladder /rmhladder/{companies,health,jobs,resume,
review,settings} /services /services/cars /spaces(28) /trash /user-builds/submit
/v(48) /ventures /wallet(28) /wrapped`

**Tested fix (not applied):** injecting
`[data-slot='page-content'] > .px-4, [data-slot='page-content'] > .px-3 { padding-inline: 0 !important }`
into all 128 pages moved **32 of the 44** to 16px with **no regressions**. The 12
that remain have a second nested wrapper and need their own edit: `/achievements
/communities /create /create/earnings /creator-studio /help /predictions /ranked
/recap /spaces /v /wallet /wrapped`.

Recommended: (a) correct the two doc recipes first, (b) fix the wrapper once in the
shell rather than editing ~100 files, (c) hand-fix the 12, (d) add a gate (the probe
is ~40 lines) so it cannot return.

### Status of §1 — fixed

Re-measured on all 128 routes after the fix: **44 doubled pages → 3, with no
regressions** (clean pages 23 → 70). What changed:

- The shell now neutralises an inline `px-3`/`px-4` on the direct child of
  `page-content` (`components/radial/radial.css`).
- The column components that render under `PageFrame` (so the shell rule cannot
  reach them) lost their own inline padding: `AchievementsColumn`,
  `WrappedColumn`, `RecapColumn`, `RankedColumn`, `SpacesColumn`, `ProgressColumn`,
  `DraftsColumn`, `FlashcardsColumn`; `RMHCoinsPage`/`WalletPage` lost a `mx-*`.
- `.cstudio-body` and its RMHVibe hero no longer add an inline inset
  (`creator-studio.css`); `--studio-gutter` is gone.
- The two doc recipes no longer prescribe `px-4`.
- `pnpm audit:pages` now reports a first card inset from the heading as a
  page-anatomy deviation, so it cannot return unnoticed.

The three that remain are judgement calls, not mechanical doubling: `/help` (a
chat panel with its own internal padding and a bordered footer strip),
and `/create` and `/v` (the prompt dock is deliberately `min(540px, 100vw - 48px)`,
a centred box 8px narrower than the column).

## 6. Bottom mini-globe lags on the Y axis when the viewport height changes

On phones the **orb is pure CSS**: `bottom: calc(--orb-bottom - --vv-shift)`
(`radial.css:1142`), so it rides Safari's fixed container. The **globe is a separate
element** centred at `top: calc(50% + --vv-shift / 2)` (`radial.css:2409`) and held
onto the orb by a JS-measured `translate: --dock-dx --dock-dy` (`measureDock`,
`RadialHub.tsx:429`). When the container height changes by Δ, the orb moves Δ and
the globe's centre moves Δ/2; the other Δ/2 is only restored the next time
`measureDock()` runs.

`measureDock()` runs on: window `resize`, a `ResizeObserver` on the dock and stage
(neither observes a _position_ change, and the dock's size uses `48svh`, which
doesn't change), and when `--vv-shift` changes value (`viewport-shift.ts` only
re-measures while the gap is non-zero). During Safari's toolbar animation the
container and visual viewport move together (gap ≈ 0), so no re-measure fires until
`resize` lands, which iOS delivers late. Result: the globe trails the orb, then
snaps.

**Reproduced the mechanism in Chromium:** viewport 844 → 760 with resize handlers
withheld left the globe exactly **42px (half of 84)** off the orb; with handlers live
the offset was 0. _Not_ reproduced on a real iPhone.

Recommended fix: make the docked Y a pure CSS function of the same anchor as the orb
(position the dock bottom-anchored so docked `--dock-dy` is constant, and use
`50dvh` for the open pose, as the orb's own open transform already does at
`radial.css:1208`). Cheaper stop-gap: re-run `measureDock` on every
`visualViewport` resize/scroll tick, not only when `--vv-shift` changes. The
withheld-resize harness above is a ready regression check for either.
