# Hearthworks brand pack: asset attributions

All third-party assets are CC0 / public domain or SIL OFL. No attribution is legally required, but credit is given here anyway.

## 1. Isometric Landscape (Kenney) - CC0
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-landscape (file `isometricLandscape.zip`, by Kenney Vleugels for www.kenney.nl)
- Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Used: grass block tile `landscapeTiles_067` (the ground of all three floating islets).
- Changes: per-pixel recoloured by hue class (grass to aqua/mint ramp, soil sides to powder-blue ramp), upscaled 1.5x, assembled into 4x3 / 3x2 isometric grids, given a 7px deep-teal (#2E7F86) silhouette outline.

## 2. Isometric Miniature Farm (Kenney) - CC0
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-miniature-farm (file `kenney_isometricminiaturefarm.zip`, Kenney, www.kenney.nl, v2.0)
- Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Used: `planks_S`, `woodWall_S/E/N/W`, `woodWallWindowGlass_N`, `woodWallDoorClosed_W`, `roofSingle_S/E`, `chimneyBase_S`, `chimneyTop_S` (the cottage); `dirtFarmland_S`, `cornDouble_S`, `cornYoungDouble_S` (crop fields); `hayBalesStacked_S` (hay).
- Changes: luminance re-mapped onto pastel blue / teal / butter ramps (wood to powder blue, roof to teal, stone to pale blue, corn to aqua-teal, hay to soft butter); cottage assembled from modular pieces; scaled to match the landscape tiles; deep-teal outline added.

## 3. Fredoka (Google Fonts) - SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fredoka (Fredoka[wdth,wght].ttf, OFL.txt; (c) 2016 The Fredoka Project Authors)
- Licence: SIL OFL 1.1, https://scripts.sil.org/OFL (modification and commercial use allowed; font is not sold by itself)
- Used: the "Hearthworks" wordmark (Bold, wght 700) and banner/card tagline text (SemiBold).
- Changes: wordmark glyphs converted to vector outlines (logo.svg has no font dependency), each letter slightly rotated and offset for a hand-made feel, and the "o" replaced with a drawn cog.

## Drawn from scratch (no third-party source)
All SVG elements were drawn for this pack: the emblem (cottage + chimney smoke + cog + leaves), trees, bushes, mushrooms, characters, cooking pot and stove, conveyor belt and crates, the factory shed, signs, baskets, clouds, cogs, the floating-island undersides, the sky and the pills.

## Not used
Kenney.nl itself returned HTTP 403 from this environment, so the Kenney packs above were obtained from their official OpenGameArt mirrors (same author, same CC0 licence). No Valve / Half-Life / Team Fortress / Counter-Strike / Portal material and no other copyrighted game art was used.
