# UI Minimalism, Consistency, Search & Layout-Shift Audit — 2026-10-09

> One pass over the **site tier** answering what the owner asked for together:
> remove unnecessary eyebrows; make tab selection more intuitive; split and
> reorganise pages where that helps; find where the site looks like several
> products; find out why "Explore everything" and search behave differently and
> why the AI lookup misses; make loads and refreshes free of layout shift and
> flicker, signed in and out; and stop the liquid globe lagging on first open.
>
> Companions: [`design-language.md`](./design-language.md) (the system, updated
> for the new tab grammar), [`page-consistency.md`](./page-consistency.md) (the
> per-page checklist, updated), [`ui-audit-2026-08-01.md`](./ui-audit-2026-08-01.md)
> (the previous site-tier pass; its findings still hold).
>
> Finding IDs are stable: `MIN-x` minimalism, `TAB-x` tabs, `ORG-x` page
> organisation, `CON-x` consistency, `SRC-x` search, `CLS-x` layout shift,
> `GLB-x` the globe.

---

## How it was measured

Everything below was observed in the running app, not inferred from source:

- **Screenshots** with Playwright (Chromium) at **1440px** and **390px**,
  **signed in and signed out**, in **Daylight, Midnight and High contrast**,
  across the nav destinations (`/`, `/explore`, `/create`, `/games`, `/library`,
  `/communities`, `/store`, `/predictions`, `/developer`, `/services`,
  `/ventures`, `/messages`, `/settings`, profiles) and the `PageFrame` routes.
- **Layout shift** on a **production build** (`vite build` + the Nitro server —
  dev mode injects CSS through JS and shifts in ways production never does): a
  `layout-shift` `PerformanceObserver` injected before any page script, recording
  each shift's score and the elements that moved, over 20 routes × 2 auth states
  × 2 widths, with real posts in the database.
- **The globe** with CDP: main-thread script/style/layout time over the 600ms
  after the tap for first vs. later opens at 4× CPU throttle, and a sampled CPU
  profile of each open.
- **Search** with a probe of the scorer over ~60 plain-word queries, and the API
  hit signed in and out.

## 1. Minimalism

### MIN-1 [fixed] "RMH STUDIOS PRESENTS" above every page title

`PageLayout` printed the same uppercase kicker above the title of ~90 routes,
directly under a top bar that already says RMH Studios. It said nothing about
the page and pushed every title down a line. Removed; the meta row now renders
only when it has a job (a back link, now in sentence case).

### MIN-2 [fixed] Second heroes under the page title

- `/create` stacked an untranslated "RMH Studios presents" over a display-size
  "The everything platform." **under** its own title and tab strip, with 12vh of
  top padding — two headlines before the one control. Now one section heading,
  "Describe a page to generate", sized like a section.
- `/store` → Membership: a "MEMBERSHIP" eyebrow over "Choose your altitude."
  repeated the selected tab. Eyebrow removed (`PinnedHero.eyebrow` is optional
  now).
- Shop, Recap and Wrapped heroes: decorative eyebrows that restated the
  headline, removed. The creator storefront's `@handle` stays — it carries
  information.
- The globe's title card: "RMH PRESENTS" kicker removed.

### MIN-3 [fixed] 39 section headings as uppercase eyebrows, in 20+ variants

Group headings inside pages ("Trending", "Who to follow", "Milestones",
"Accent color") were hand-typed as tiny uppercase tracked labels —
`tracking-wide` vs `-wider`, `dim` vs `muted`, `xs` vs `sm`, semibold vs bold.
There is one style now, **`.site-section-label`** (sentence case, semibold, full
ink), and **`design-consistency.test.ts`** fails a site-tier `h2`/`h3` carrying
`uppercase`. Five brand/editorial microsites whose small caps are their own voice
are allowlisted (one-directional).

### MIN-4 [fixed] "Explore everything"

A full-width outlined pill with the **Sparkles** glyph — the site's AI icon — at
the foot of the live rail on every page, including `/explore` itself and an empty
rail. It read as a separate AI feature; all it did was open the nav's Explore
page. Now a quiet "More on Explore →" footer link, shown only under the Explore
previews it belongs to and never on `/explore`.

## 2. Tab selection

### TAB-1 [fixed] Selection looked like an action

The active tab was a solid accent pill. On the default theme that is a black pill
with white ink — exactly what "New", "Create a community" and "Sign in" are. A
page with a tab strip and a primary action showed two identical black pills with
opposite meanings.

`LiquidTabs` is now **Apple's segmented control**: a neutral raised **thumb**
(opaque surface, hairline, small shadow) on a recessed `.glass-inset` **track**,
label in full ink. The accent fill is for actions. High contrast keeps the filled
accent thumb (the material is gone there, so a hairline thumb would be the only
cue). The thumb paint is `--tab-thumb-*` custom properties, so Slice It! re-skins
it with variables instead of overriding internals. Checked in all three themes.

### TAB-2 [fixed] Truncated labels and button-grid wrapping

The strip was a CSS grid of equal columns that wrapped when it ran out of room.
In practice equal columns ellipsed long labels long before wrapping —
`/services`: "RMHH…", "RMHL…", "Rebar …"; `/library`: "Everyt…", "Collect…" —
**at 1440px** — and on a phone six tabs became a 2×3 grid of identical buttons
that no longer read as tabs and cost ~150px of the first screen.

Now: **one row, labels never truncate.** ≤4 tabs split the track equally; more
size to their labels; a strip that still overflows **scrolls with an edge fade**
on whichever side has more, and keeps the active tab in view (by moving the
strip's own scroll offset, so the page never jumps).

### TAB-3 [fixed] Hand-rolled switcher on /predictions

Open / Resolved / Mine was a row of tinted buttons with no tab semantics and a
third switcher look on the page. It is `LiquidTabs` (small) now.

## 3. Page organisation

### ORG-1 [fixed] /services and /ventures hid their contents behind tabs

Each hub showed one product at a time behind a strip of product names (which a
segmented control cannot hold — TAB-2). A hub's job is to show what is there:
both are **lists** now, built from one shared `HubLinkRow` (icon, name, one line,
the whole card is the link).

### ORG-2 [fixed] The 3D showcases got their own pages

RMH Cars and RMH Fashion were tab panels of `/services`. They are content in their
own right, so they moved to **`/services/cars`** and **`/services/fashion`** with
their own title, canonical and sitemap entry. `/services?tab=cars|fashion`
redirects; other old `?tab=` links open the list.

### ORG-3 [fixed] /games put the Arcade Pass above the games

Signed out, `/games` showed Ranked plus a full Arcade Pass panel (its own tab
strip and a "sign in to play" block) before any game — the code comment claimed
the opposite. The Arcade Pass now follows the catalog; the compact party bar and
Ranked line stay on top.

### ORG-4 [fixed] /communities spent a row on one button

Embedded as a tab, the column rendered a title-less header holding only "New",
a hairline, the search field, and another hairline. Search and "New" now share
one toolbar row, like the Inbox.

## 4. Consistency

### CON-1 [fixed] Two page-title styles

~90 routes title themselves with `PageLayout` (flat display type on the
backdrop). The ~25 `PageFrame` routes — Bookmarks/Saved, Drafts, Ranked, a tag
page, Flashcards, Progress, Leaderboard … — used `ColumnHeader`: an icon and a
`text-2xl` title in a glass capsule. Same site, two faces, sizes and containers
for "this is the page you are on". `ColumnHeader` now renders `PageLayout`'s own
header markup whenever it is the page's `h1`; embedded `h2` uses and
search-style headers keep the compact row. `/help` had a third, hand-rolled
capsule with an untranslated "Help"; it uses the shared header now.

### CON-2 [fixed] Smaller drift

- `/library`'s header action was a bespoke pill; it is the shared `Button`.
- `/predictions`' market loader was a hand-rolled `Loader2`; it is `Spinner`.

### CON-3 [open] Phone header actions collapse to a lone glyph

`globals.css` deliberately collapses `[data-slot='page-header-action']` buttons
to a 44px glyph under 480px, and the header then wraps it onto its own row under
the lede (visible on `/library`). It is a site-wide decision, so it was left
alone here; the better shape is probably to keep the glyph on the title's row.

## 5. Search

The three search surfaces — the `/explore` page, the top-bar search panel and the
⌘K palette — were **three different searches**, which is why they disagreed.

### SRC-1 [fixed] The top-bar panel only knew people and posts

It asked `/api/search` for the legacy `type=all` shape and rendered only the
`people` and `posts` arrays, so a game, app or page searched **by its exact
name** said "No matches yet" there while Explore listed it first. It now asks for
the ranked cross-kind `tab=top` list and renders it with Explore's per-kind
vocabulary, showing only confident hits when there are any.

### SRC-2 [fixed] ⌘K ranked with a different engine

The palette used Fuse.js; Explore and the top bar use `lib/search/score.ts`. It
uses the shared scorer now, so all three rank identically.

### SRC-3 [fixed] Signed-out search returned "No results"

`/api/search` 401'd signed-out visitors and every surface rendered that as "No
results" — a visitor typing a game's exact name on `/explore` was told it did not
exist. Signed-out requests are now answered from the **static catalog only**
(games, apps, pages: in-memory, no DB or model cost). People, posts and the
document corpora still require a session (verified: a signed-out people search
returns nothing for an existing user), the model-assisted retry never runs
signed out, and Explore says what a signed-out search covers.

### SRC-4 [fixed] Why the AI lookup "doesn't always work"

1. **Its timeout was shorter than the model.** Query expansion raced DeepSeek
   against **1.5s** — less than a typical completion — so most weak queries
   resolved to "no expansion" and cached that for a minute. The assist is a
   separate request that fires after typing stops, with results already on
   screen, so it now waits **4s**; nothing visible moves until something better
   arrives.
2. **The model was told nothing about the site.** It could only guess generic
   synonyms. It now receives the catalog's names and is asked to put an exact
   match first; the retry tries that before the spelling correction.

### SRC-5 [fixed] Matching noise and holes

- **Typo tolerance was too loose:** two edits with a different first letter
  counted as a typo, so "racing" matched the **Pricing** page at 0.68 (above
  the medium band). A second edit now requires the first letters to agree and
  costs a little more; one-edit typos are unaffected.
- **"Did you mean"** scored whole records against a 0.22 floor — "audit"
  suggested "Spaces", "hello" suggested "Dream Rift". It now scores titles only,
  at 0.5; real slips ("tournamnet", "rmhtbe", "slce it") still correct.
- **Coverage:** 20 real destinations that plain-word queries could not reach —
  "tournament", "rideshare", "dark mode", "blackjack", "services", "events",
  "spaces", "developer api", … — are in `SITE_DESTINATIONS` now (which also feeds
  ⌘K). Regression cases are in `lib/search/__tests__/catalog.test.ts`.

## 6. Layout shift & flicker

Every non-zero source was chased down, on a production build, on loads and on
client navigations:

| ID | Where | Cause | Before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLS-1 | Home feed, signed in | The lazy composer's placeholder was ~40px shorter than the composer and laid out differently; then a 6px baseline gap from an `inline-block` textarea | 0.033 (390px) / 0.0084 (1440px) → **0** |
| CLS-2 | Profile header | The achievement strip was fetched after hydration and grew the header 54px | 0.0061 → **0** |
| CLS-3 | Live rail (every page ≥1440px) | "N people online" rendered nothing until its fetch resolved, then pushed every card down 47px | 0.0034 → **0** |
| CLS-4 | Live rail (every page ≥1440px) | Each ambient widget (online pill, Today, Friends online, Trending, Who to follow) rendered the moment its own fetch landed, so later arrivals inserted themselves above earlier ones | revealed in one piece (below) |
| CLS-5 | Live rail, on navigation | The page's own rail content sat ABOVE the ambient widgets, so moving between a page with rail content (a profile, ~850px) and one without moved every widget by that much | **0.099 → 0** |

CLS-4 and CLS-5 were invisible to the first measurement because the local
database was empty — no trending tags, no one to suggest — so the rail barely
rendered. They were re-measured with seeded trending posts and suggested users,
on page loads and on client navigations (recording shifts the CLS metric itself
discounts as input-driven, since a jump you see after a click is still a jump).
The fix: the ambient column comes first and the page's contribution after it, so
swapping the page part moves nothing above it; the ambient column is laid out
invisibly while its widgets fill and revealed once, with a 3s backstop; the page
part waits for it on first load only; and Friends online — which changes live
every minute — sits below the steady cards. After it: **0** on every load (20
routes × 3 widths × signed in/out) and every navigation tested.

Related flicker fixed with CLS-1: the composer avatar went *initial → empty
circle → image* on every load (`loading="lazy"` above the fold); it is eager now.
The composer placeholder's character budget comes from a new dependency-free
`lib/rmhark-limits.ts`, so it does not pull zod into the feed's first chunk.

## 7. The globe's first-open lag

### GLB-1 [fixed] The first open was the first time for everything

Preloading the globe's chunk on idle was already in place, so the download was
not the cost. What still happened for the first time *inside* the 500ms open
morph was everything else: the globe's code paths compiling on first call, its
first React mount, its canvas backing store being allocated, its layers
rasterising, and the GPU setting up the programs for the page blur and the
veil's circular mask. Every later open reuses all of that, which is exactly why
only the first one hitched.

The hub now **rehearses** the open once per page load, while the page is idle:
right after the chunk preloads it mounts the globe for ~600ms — fixed, centred
like the real overlay, at 1% opacity, `inert`, no pointer events, haptics off —
and turns it half a revolution on its settle spring, so the projection, magnet
and paint code run hot rather than painting once and going idle. Beside it, a
tiny element uses the same blur radius and circular mask to warm those GPU
programs. Then it unmounts.

Measured on the production build at 4× CPU throttle (median of 6 page loads):
the first open's extra script time over a later open went from **~+60ms** to
**~+21ms** (roughly 5ms on a desktop). A frame diff taken during and after the
rehearsal shows no change in the centre of the screen.

### GLB-2 [kept] The page blur behind the globe

An intermediate version of this pass replaced the page blur with hiding the feed
behind the veil. That was a design regression and was reverted: the blur behind
the globe is exactly as it was. (Two things learned on the way: `opacity` on
`.radial-frame` does not reach the wheel's 3D-transformed cards, and in this
software-GPU environment blurred and unblurred opens cost the same — the first-open
cost was never the blur's steady state, it was everything happening for the
first time.)

## 8. What is enforced now

- `design-consistency.test.ts` — **no uppercase `h2`/`h3` on the site tier**
  (`.site-section-label` instead), with a one-directional allowlist.
- `lib/search/__tests__/catalog.test.ts` — the coverage and noise regressions in
  SRC-5.
- `i18n-catalogs.test.ts` / `fouc-contract.test.ts` — the new English-only keys
  and the two reviewed `lazy()` boundaries (`/services/cars`, `/services/fashion`).

New English strings are listed in `KNOWN_UNTRANSLATED`; the translate pipeline
needs `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`, which was not available here.
