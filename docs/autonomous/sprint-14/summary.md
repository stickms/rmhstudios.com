# Sprint 14 — summary

Twenty features shipped on `claude/autonomous-sprint-14`. Each was built from its brief in its own worktree, reviewed by a fresh reviewer who fixed what they found, and merged wave by wave (plan: [plan.md](plan.md)).

## What shipped
- **01 Identity Crisis.** The RMHBox stub is a playable minigame. Everyone sees everybody's identity but their own, takes turns asking the table a yes-or-no question, and the table votes yes, no or maybe, shown as counts only. Guesses are matched on the server with fuse.js: an early correct guess scores 200 plus up to 300 for unused question slots, a wrong one costs 100 and ends the player's questions, and the final guess scores 200. The spec's voting-accuracy bonus was dropped on purpose, because the server cannot know the true answer to a free-text question. Secrecy is built from allowlists: a player's state never carries their own identity before the reveal, the asker gets a turn message without it, guess text reaches only the guesser and spectators, and votes are never sent or logged. Questions are member text (3 to 200 characters, control characters stripped, `dir="auto"`). 298 bundled identities ship. Review took the game number from the lobby's match history instead of a module-level map that was never cleaned, and removed three identities likelier to hurt than amuse.
- **02 Your Hearthworks town on every device.** The town, which lived only in IndexedDB, is also kept as a gzipped copy on the shared save table. The local copy is written first; the account copy follows at most every five minutes and only when the world moved on, and a `pagehide` beacon is sent only when the body fits the 64 KB beacon cap. A guest makes no request. A member with a local-only town is offered "Save this town to your account", and when both copies moved on, the shared conflict dialog asks which to keep before anything is overwritten. The save `POST` gains an optional `expectedUpdatedAt` compare-and-set (409 with the stored time, nothing written), which Hearthworks always sends and the other games do not. IndexedDB stamps the owning account, so another member's town is never offered. Review dropped every seat but the member's own from the uploaded world, so past guests' ids and money never reach the account copy or the export.
- **03 Your Synapse Storm matches.** "Your matches" in the multiplayer menu lists each finished versus match with the member's place, score, best combo, solved and missed, and the other players by their current names ("A player" for guests, blocks either way and bans; "Deleted user" for erased members). It is keyset paged 20 at a time on the member's `(lastUpdateAt, id)`, served by a new `CONCURRENTLY` index with its `_ensure_valid` repair, and says once that versus scores are reported by each player's game. The rows have no `User` relation, so erasure tombstones them in place: the id becomes `deleted:<row id>` (which keeps both unique keys) and the name "Deleted user". The export carries `synapseMatches` with a player count and no other player's name. Review replaced one `updateMany` per row with three set-based statements inside the erasure transaction.
- **04 Your Altair co-op record, and the co-op boards.** "Your runs" shows the co-op record (runs, wins and win rate, revives given and received, kills) above the co-op list, with the most-played class computed from `AltairMatchPlayer` rather than the stored `favoriteClassId`. The leaderboard gains `coop-wins` and `coop-revives`, ordered by the indexed column with the row id as tie-break, cut to ten, and leaving out hidden and banned members through `userNotHiddenWhere`. The export carries the totals as `sprint14.altairCoop`. Review listed only members with a win or a revive, and marked the active category with `aria-pressed`.
- **05 Where your Doctrine XP came from.** `/strategies/profile/xp` shows the last 30 days by action and then every change newest first, keyset paged on `(createdAt, id)`. The profile heatmap, which was passed `{}`, is fed from the same ledger in the browser's time zone, and "Full XP breakdown" opens the new page instead of the leaderboard. The reputation and ledger join the export. The stored reason and metadata are never selected, so no puzzle or incident id leaves the server. Review ordered the capped 30-day and heatmap reads newest first, so the 20,000-row cap drops the oldest rows, and gave the heatmap a screen-reader summary.
- **06 Report a broken daily puzzle, and check the coming days.** A member who finished an AI puzzle can send one report per puzzle (four kinds and a note, replaceable). `/admin/daily-puzzles` shows each mode's next two days and the last week with answers and reports, generates a missing future day early, and replaces a day that has not started and has no scores. The replace generates with no lock held, then re-checks the window, the 10-minute margin, the scores and the row the admin saw inside one transaction under a per-day advisory lock, and updates that row in place, so the unique `[gameMode, dateKey]` never has a gap (`PUZZLE_CHANGED`, `PUZZLE_ALREADY_STARTED`). Reports are exported and erased. Review made the score route refuse a day after today in New York, so no score can land on a day Replace may still change.
- **07 Report a Slice It song, or a comment on one.** "Report song" in a song's details and "Report" on others' comments open the site's report dialog, loaded lazily. Both types are removable: a song goes through the existing final takedown (audio deleted; row, charts and scores kept) and a comment is deleted. A private, taken-down or missing song gives one refusal, and nobody can report their own. The queue previews a song's title, artist and uploader and a comment's text. Review added "This deletes the song's audio. It cannot be undone." to the removal confirm.
- **08 Report a game review, a guide, a discussion or an achievement tip.** Each of the five kinds of member text on a game's page has a "Report" item for anyone but its author. "Resolve and remove" stamps a review, guide or tip with `moderatedAt`, or deletes a discussion or reply through the hub's own admin delete, which counts the thread's replies down. A removed review, guide or tip leaves the game page, the hub, the profile count, the activity feed, the sitemap, the guide fan-out, awards, tip lists and the rating (its cache is dropped at once). Its author still sees it marked "Removed by a moderator" and cannot edit, delete, publish or restore it. `GAME_CONTENT_READ_PATHS` and a source scan hold every reader. The user-text exemption for all of `components/arcade/` was narrowed, since achievement tips are member text. Review gave an edit or delete of a removed item its own refusal (`GAME_CONTENT_REMOVED_BY_MODERATOR`) and made a thread already deleted by a second decision count as done.
- **09 Mute, block or report from a profile, and report an impersonator.** A profile has a menu beside Follow: Mute (with durations), Block, Report and "Pretending to be someone", for a signed-in member on someone else's profile, and the rows read Unmute and Unblock from the profile read. The impersonation endpoint, which no screen called, moves onto a contract and takes the impersonated account as a handle or as "Me", with coded refusals; the generic report route refuses the type, so this endpoint, with its rate limit and dedupe, is the only way in. The queue labels the group "Impersonation", previews who is being copied, opens the two accounts side by side, and lists the other accounts claiming the reported account's websites through a new admin endpoint. `accountsClaimingHost` now sorts verified claims first with nulls last. Review put the one-open-report check and the insert in one transaction under an advisory lock on the pair.
- **10 Report an RMHTube room or watch party.** A `tube-room` report is filed from the room header and the party page. "Resolve and remove" stamps `RmhTubeRoom.moderatedAt` (and `closedAt`, never overwriting an earlier close) in one conditional update, so only the winning moderator tells the members going and publishes on a new Redis channel. The hub ends the running room (`room:closed`, reason `moderator`) and neither loader brings it back. Every reader leaves a removed room out, held by `TUBE_ROOM_READ_PATHS` and a source scan. The host cannot edit it back, or schedule, rename or create a live room with a name removed from their rooms in the last 30 days, in the web tier and in the hub. Review normalised names the same way on both sides (markup stripped, cut to 64, trimmed, lower-cased), made the hub refuse when the removed-name read fails, and unsubscribed the channel at shutdown.
- **11 Hear about replies to your game threads and comments on your songs.** `notifyThreadReply` replaces `notifyHubReply`, which told only the thread's author, in English, with no block or mute check. The author and up to 50 earlier posters get "{{name}} replied in {{title}}" as one REPLY row (the same entity, so an old unread row is refreshed, not doubled). Blocks either way, mutes, bans and pending deletions are skipped, and the thread page's new "Mute this thread" mutes a `game-thread` conversation scope. `notifySongComment` tells the uploader of a public song. Both run after the write and log rather than fail it. Review ordered recipients by each author's newest post (`groupBy` with `_max createdAt`, not `distinct`), led the inbox row with the thread notice, and removed a deleted thread's unread notices.
- **12 Pin conversations to the top, and see which you muted.** The default Messages view starts its first page with up to five pinned conversations and leaves their ids out of the paged query on every page. The pin write runs under a per-member advisory lock, so two concurrent sixth pins leave five; it refuses a chat the caller is not in, and clears pins of chats no longer visible. Rows carry Pinned and Muted marks from one batched preference read per page, and the conversation header gets "Pin to top" beside the mute control. Review replaced Prisma's id cursor with an explicit `(lastMessageAt, id)` keyset, because a cursor row pinned between pages fell outside the where and equal times had no tie-break.
- **13 See which of the people replying to you support you.** The reply list carries `supporter: true` on replies from the post author's active supporters, and `CommentItem` shows "Supporter" in `t-meta` after the name. Only the post's author, with the new profile switch on (`UserProfile.showSupporterMarks`, default on, additive migration), ever gets the field; for everyone else the key is absent, and the route keeps no cache policy. With the switch off no membership lookup is made. Thread replies are not covered.
- **14 Edit a game jam, and its theme shortlist, after creating it.** `PATCH /api/jams/$slug` edits a jam within what its phase allows: everything but the slug before the start (a voting jam's theme stays the vote's result), and after it only the description and later deadlines. The phase and the time are read inside the transaction under the jam's lock and then the shortlist's, so two saves serialise and a phase that moved since the dialog opened is refused. Each save writes one audit row naming the changed fields, never the secret theme. The date-order rule is one pure function shared with `createJam`, with refusal codes. The jam page gets "Edit jam" and, for a voting jam before its start, "Edit shortlist", which finally calls the existing shortlist contract. Review coded the shortlist refusals (`JAM_SHORTLIST_LOCKED`, `JAM_EDIT_LOCKED`).
- **15 Saved homes and saved searches in the developer API.** Two explicit scopes, `read:homes` and `write:homes`, that no wildcard or default grant reaches, read saved homes (note, price alert, newest price changes) and saved searches, and save, annotate or unsave a home through the site's own `favorite`, `setFavoriteNote`, `setPriceAlerts` and `unfavorite`. The API adds the block check (a blocked author is a 404). A removed listing, or one by a blocked author, reads `available: false` with its figures null, and no response carries an address, coordinates, a search's centre or `priceNoticeAt`. It also fixed the duplicate `completedAt` key in the SDK generator's `NULLABLE` map (TS1117) and the docs slug shape. Review regenerated the site reference.
- **16 Self-exclusion hides the coin games, as it promises.** `RISK_SURFACE_PATHS` names the surfaces, a private `GET /api/settings/play-limits/surfaces` answers one `hidden` bit (self-exclusion only), and `useRiskSurfacesHidden()` reads it for the sidebar, the globe, the account menu, `/sections`, the layout editor, the command palette, the wallet shortcut and play button, the profile coin count, the elections links and a casino table's invite. Search drops the pages on the server. `/predictions`, `/wager/$id` and the Compete hub's Wagers tab are decided in the route loader, so the notice is in the HTML with scripting off and the open wagers are not fetched. `RISK_SURFACE_LINKERS` fails a file that links a risk surface without being listed. Review found that wager holds and tournament entry fees moved coins through `lib/wager/escrow.server.ts`'s own conditional update and never ran the play-limit check, so an excluded member could still stake from a direct request: `debitStake` now runs `assertPlayAllowedOn` inside the same transaction, and the two routes answer the 403 codes. Review also kept the coin games out of `/api/ai/search` and its model-assisted correction.
- **17 A timeout that holds on every reply and every chat.** The active-timeout rule moved to `restrictions-core.ts`, which the hubs can import (injected client, 30 s cache). `restriction-paths.ts` lists every enforcement path and every writer of member text, held by two source scans. The comment check now covers builds, build issues, song comments, chart and game reviews, guides, game threads and posts, achievement tips, guestbooks, jam feedback, prediction comments, RMHTube timed comments and commentary, and deck suggestions; the message check covers ride chat, the RMHTube, RMHBox and RMHStudy chats and the Identity Crisis ask. `POST /api/v1/posts` and its comments answer 403 `feature_restricted`, and `publishScheduledPost` leaves a draft a draft while the author is timed out. Review closed the gaps it found and fixed the gates the first pass bent: chat thread replies, checklists, slash commands, forwards, DM edits and Space chat, context notes and the requests board now check, and five models join `MEMBER_TEXT_MODELS`; the hub cache is an `lruL1()` from a new plain `lib/cache-l1.ts` instead of a hand-rolled class that sidestepped the ad-hoc cache ratchet; a hub's fail-open read is capped at 1.5 s and skips reads for 5 s after a failure; and the due-post sweep excludes timed-out authors in the query (`activeRestrictionRule`), so one member's queued drafts cannot fill every batch.
- **18 Erasure that sees every member column, not only relations.** `PLAIN_MEMBER_COLUMNS` classifies the 42 models a schema scan finds with a member id in a plain column (the specification's 37 plus five the wider `*ById` pattern adds), and `MEMBER_COLUMN_PATTERN` is the one list of names. A sibling coverage test fails a model with a matching column and no entry, an erase or deidentify entry that `anonymizeAccount` does not carry out, a cascade whose parent is not erased, and a stale docs page. `plainColumnErasureOps` adds the missing operations: optional columns are nulled, required ones take the deleted-user id. A security report's reporter id, name, e-mail and address hash, chat removals, Doctrine incident reporters, RMHBox and RMHType winners and the authors of staff content are now erased. Checked against the readers, three defaults changed (`EventUpdate`, `CommunityMemberNote`, `StudioProjectRevision`). Review classified `Subscription.referenceId` (the Stripe plugin's billing row, kept) and added `hostId`, `senderId` and `recipientId` to the pattern.
- **19 Test doubles that know the schema.** `lib/db/query-shape.ts` (test-time only) builds a model map from the schema source, cross-checked against `Prisma.dmmf`, and rejects unknown fields, null comparisons and `nulls` ordering on required columns, nested selects of missing fields, `select` with `include`, unknown data fields and unknown operations. `shapeChecked` wraps any fake client (and the transaction client), and `assertJsonSafe` rejects BigInt, functions, cycles and non-finite numbers. The shared economy fake and the sprint 13 tests that had hidden runtime failures adopted it. Adopting it found a live bug, fixed in `9e3c3066e`: the RMHLadder AI enrichment read ordered by a column `LadderJob` does not have. Review kept fakes' spies intact through the wrapper (a `Proxy` apply trap) and removed two false alarms.
- **20 Close the gaps sprint 13 left open.** A resolved report brings back a pack the reports auto-hid. A group thread takedown lowers the parent's `replyCount` in the same transaction, never below zero. The RMHTube composer drops a reply target a moderator removed. `/admin/deletions` uses count plurals ("1 account") and mutes the no-failure line. Altair builds no `Howl`, and so no `AudioContext`, before the first pointer or key event, and "Your runs" focuses its heading on open. Vega spends the assist claim before judging the run, so a refused run leaves none behind. Ranked tier names are sent with a stable id and translated in the reader's language. Review pluralised the Finished erasure counts and tested that the audio gesture listeners are removed. Not taken: S13-09, 12, 14, 15, 17's concurrent edit, 18 and 19 (the spec says why).
