# M03 — LLM slow or failing

**Sev3**, one alarm per provider and call type (e.g. `latency:deepseek:chat`, `errors:xai:image`):

- **`latency:*`**: p95 response time over 30 seconds across 10 minutes.
- **`errors:*`**: more than 20% of calls failed (non-2xx or network error).

1. Provider status page (DeepSeek, xAI). Most of these are upstream.
2. `docker logs <web container> --since 30m | grep '\[ai\]'`: our side of the errors (key, quota, timeouts).
3. Features degrade to their fallbacks; nothing to roll back unless a deploy changed prompts or models.
