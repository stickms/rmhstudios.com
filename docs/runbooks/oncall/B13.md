# B13 — Background job failing / backlog

**Sev3.** Instances:

- **`B13:<queue>`**: 5 or more new failures on a non-money queue in 15 minutes.
- **`B13:<queue>:backlog`**: more than 500 jobs ready but not picked up, for 15 minutes.

1. Backlog: is the jobs worker running ([A16](A16.md))? Is one handler stuck (logs)?
2. Failures: `docker logs <jobs container> --since 30m | grep <queue>`.
