# B02 — Heap pressure

**Sev3.** A service's V8 heap has been above 85% of its limit for 10 minutes; it's heading for an out-of-memory restart.

1. `/admin/performance` → the process's heap and cache sizes.
2. Growing steadily over hours = a leak; note the version and open an issue. A restart buys time: `docker compose ... restart <service>`.
