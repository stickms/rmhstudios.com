Historical school reports

MDApplicants launched in 2003 as a place for medical school applicants to share their backgrounds, applications and outcomes. It later became a free Student Doctor Network resource, and its historical school summaries are now preserved in CycleTrack.

These reports cover application submission years 1998-2024 across 186 medical schools. The years available vary by school.
